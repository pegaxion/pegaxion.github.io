Welcome to Pegaxion ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy when you use our mobile applications and digital services across Google Play and the Apple App Store.

Core Principle: Pegaxion builds privacy-first applications. We do not sell your personal data, nor do we track you across third-party websites without your consent.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on which Pegaxion application you use, we may collect or process the following categories of information:

  • Information You Provide Voluntarily: Account credentials (if applicable), profile details, feedback, or customer support inquiries sent directly to our email.
  • Device and Usage Information: Device model, operating system version, anonymous device identifiers, language settings, and general diagnostic/crash reports to optimize app stability.
  • Locally Stored Data: For offline-first utilities and productivity apps, your data (notes, tasks, inputs) is stored securely on your local device storage unless you explicitly choose to sync via cloud backup.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely to:

  • Provide, maintain, and enhance the functionality of our mobile applications.
  • Detect, prevent, and fix technical bugs, crashes, or security vulnerabilities.
  • Respond promptly to support requests and customer feedback.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and store policies.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Our applications may integrate trusted third-party SDKs to provide analytics, crash reporting, or monetization. These providers process data in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services / Firebase: For analytics and crash reporting.
  • Apple App Store Services: For in-app purchase validation and distribution analytics.

4. Data Retention & Deletion (Your Rights)

You retain full control over your data. In compliance with Google Play and Apple App Store policies, GDPR, and CCPA:

  • You may request complete deletion of any stored data associated with your account at any time.
  • To request account or data deletion, simply email us at pegaxion@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will process and confirm your request within 7 business days.

5. Children's Privacy

Our applications do not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If we learn that we have inadvertently gathered personal data from a child, we will take prompt steps to delete that information.

6. Security of Your Data

We implement standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.
