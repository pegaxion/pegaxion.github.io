How to Request Account & Data Deletion

In adherence to Google Play and Apple App Store user data safety policies, any user can permanently delete their account and associated data.

  1. Send an email to pegaxion@gmail.com.
  2. Set the subject line to: Account Deletion Request - [App Name].
  3. Include your registered account identifier or username.

What happens next: We will permanently purge your user profile, records, and related identifiers from our systems within 7 business days and confirm by email.

Common Troubleshooting

  • In-App Purchases / Subscriptions: Managed securely by Google Play Billing or Apple App Store. To restore purchases, tap "Restore Purchases" in the app's settings.
  • Crashes or Display Glitches: Ensure you are running the latest app version available on Google Play or the App Store.
